FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

How long does a typical Shopify project take?

Most projects run between 4 and 10 weeks, depending on scope. A theme customization typically takes 2–3 weeks, a custom theme build 6–8 weeks, and a full Shopify Plus migration 8–12 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline before we begin.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. The majority of my clients are based in the US, UK, EU, and Australia. I work asynchronously across time zones with clear communication via Slack, email, and scheduled video calls.

What's included in post-launch support?

Every project includes a post-launch support period (2–6 weeks depending on tier) covering bug fixes, minor adjustments, and performance monitoring. For ongoing needs, I offer monthly retainer plans.

Can you work with my existing designer?

Yes — I frequently collaborate with in-house teams and external designers. I'm comfortable working from Figma files, design systems, or even rough wireframes. Clear specs and organized assets make for the smoothest collaboration.

Do you offer Shopify Plus services?

Yes. I have experience with Shopify Plus including checkout extensibility, script editor, launchpad, flow automation, and multi-market setups. Enterprise-tier pricing applies for Plus-specific work.

What's your payment structure?

Projects are typically split into three milestones: 40% to begin, 30% at design approval, and 30% at launch. For larger engagements, I offer monthly billing. All payments are processed securely via bank transfer or Stripe.
