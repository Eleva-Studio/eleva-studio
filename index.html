FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects run between 2 and 6 weeks, depending on scope. A basic setup takes 1-2 weeks, while custom Liquid development and advanced theming may take 4-6 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline before we begin.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. I work asynchronously across time zones with clear communication via email, Slack, and scheduled video calls.

Can you work with my existing designer?

Yes. I frequently collaborate with external designers. I'm comfortable working from Figma files, design systems, or wireframes. Clear specs make for the smoothest collaboration.

What is your payment structure?

Projects are typically split into milestones: 50% to begin, and 50% at launch. For larger engagements, we can discuss a custom billing structure. Payments are processed securely via bank transfer or Stripe.
