About Me

A developer who treats every pixel like a promise.

I'm Aram — a Shopify developer and manager with 8+ years of experience building stores that feel like flagship boutiques, not templates.

From Yerevan to the world, I've partnered with fashion houses, DTC brands, and luxury retailers to transform their Shopify stores into revenue machines wrapped in editorial design.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering precision and creative direction — where Liquid meets typography, and conversion meets craft.

My Journey

From my first HTML page in 2016 to leading Shopify builds for international brands — every project sharpened my eye for detail.

My Mission

To build Shopify stores that don't just sell — they seduce. Every interaction should feel inevitable, elegant, and intentional.

My Vision

A future where every Shopify store feels like a piece of art — where commerce and craft are inseparable.
